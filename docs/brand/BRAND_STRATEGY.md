**Brand strategy 1.0** · Office of the Founder · Editorial implementation: Nora

The owner accepts the overall direction. These editorial standards operate within [real project governance](../GOVERNANCE.md); see [status](../STATUS.md) for release decisions.

> The future is horizontal.

## 2. Strategic pillars

| Pillar | Meaning | Expression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bed-Led Leadership™ | Executive authority without compulsory verticality | Beds, duvets, long horizontal rules, founder statements |
| Strategic Executive Non-Intervention™ | Delegate operations and retain decisions | Autopilot, governance copy, deliberate deferral |
| Outcome-Neutral Performance™ | Apparent progress with minimal actual value | Contrasting metrics, fictional reports and dashboards |
| Horizontal scale | Expand the enterprise without expanding the founder's movement | Organisational charts and campaign progression |
| Compliance-grade inactivity | Make absurdity look procedurally defensible | Clearly fictional certificates, audit language and formal records |

## 3. Audience and proposition

Primary: simulation and management-game players who enjoy systemic consequences, progression and dry humour. Secondary: developers, enterprise-software users, management-satire readers and people fatigued by productivity theatre.

Lead with the game and its concrete mechanics. Introduce the book and corporate lore as optional depth. Do not imply an actual business service, real certification, financial return, career qualification or evidence-based therapeutic outcome.

## 4. Brand architecture

NOTHING™ is the fictional parent institution. Corporate Simulator 2026™ is the playable product. The Art of Doing Nothing™ is the Founder's companion publication. Executive Library is the reading experience. Executive Autopilot, NOTHING™ OS and the Executive Career Portfolio are product features, not separately marketed services. The Founder's voice supplies doctrine; Nora supplies operational evidence.

## 5. Visual identity

| Colour | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Midnight Navy | #0B1A2B | Brand foundation and dark graphic background |
| Executive Gold | #C8A85B | Display titles, rules and ceremonial accents |
| Paper Cream | #F6F1E4 | Editorial panels and light text on navy |
| Audit Charcoal | #2E3237 | Text on light panels |
| Muted Teal | #5D8F86 | Charts and secondary visual accents |

Use cream or white for small text on navy; charcoal on cream. Gold and teal are accents, not default small text on cream. Verify contrast before changes to actual gameplay UI; never communicate status by colour alone.

Typography: system sans-serif for instructions and UI; Georgia or a comparable system editorial serif for titles and founder quotations. Use no external font dependencies. Preserve readable sizes and normal sentence case. Reserve tracked uppercase for small ceremonial labels.

The wordmark is NOTHING™ in uppercase sans-serif with a restrained gold horizontal rule. Allow generous clear space; do not distort it, add gradients to letters or place it over visually busy material. Use the full product title when a new reader needs context.

Motifs: horizontal rules, executive dashboards, organisational charts, duvet silhouettes, annual-report covers, simple bed emblems, and fictional internal accreditation seals. Avoid third-party logos, official certification marks and misleading screenshots. The illustrative graphics show fictional concepts, not measured game performance.

## 6. Editorial voice

The Founder: grandiose, buzzword-rich, sincerely committed to doing nothing. Favoured terms include strategic alignment, delegated accountability, outcome-neutral excellence, horizontal scalability and duvet utilisation.

Nora: precise, dry, accountable, concerned with evidence. State what passed, what remains pending and who owns the next decision. The humour comes from the difference between real delivery and fictional doctrine.

Game narration: serious corporate language applied to absurd events. Technical documentation: plain instructions first, satire second. Use British English. Do not joke about real private information or imply fictitious claims are independently verified.

Examples:

| Context | Approved direction |
| --- | --- |
| Play invitation | Begin your executive appointment |
| Bug report | Operational incident submission |
| Feature proposal | Strategic transformation proposal |
| Release notes | Statements of alleged progress |
| Technical failure | State the actual error, then add optional satirical context |
| Founder quote | The future is horizontal. |

## 7. Repository and distribution design

README: hero banner, concise game description, clear run instructions, gameplay features, Founder and Executive Library banners, literature links, real quality gates and publication/attribution status. Keep all essential information in Markdown text rather than images alone.

The repository-local SVG set under this directory is self-contained, accessible through alt text, and intentionally small. The more elaborate generated campaign artwork is a separate visual concept set; it is not an actual gameplay capture or replacement for the original book cover. Add it only after its files are imported and reviewed. Prefer compressed raster formats for photographic art, vector graphics for logos and simple illustrations, relative URLs, and no external image tracking.

Future collateral: GitHub social preview, verified gameplay screenshots, release cards, fictional annual reports, achievement graphics and book promotion. Each should use the same palette and hierarchy. Maintain a text equivalent for important content. Avoid a global game UI redesign unless separately justified.

## 8. Brand governance and launch

The Founder owns strategic positioning and publication decisions. Nora maintains consistency, factual technical claims, accessible presentation and asset provenance records. Record actual release status separately from fictional metrics. Maintain the manuscript's existing text and original cover unless the owner approves an editorial change.

Before public release: confirm rights and licence scope; inspect artwork and metadata; check local image links, alt text, mobile readability and dark/light GitHub rendering; verify the release commit; obtain owner approval. Do not represent fictional internal seals as external certification.

## 9. Success criteria

A new reader can identify the game, understand its objective and run it without reading the lore. A returning reader can recognise the visual system and both narrative voices. Documentation remains truthful, readable and useful. Branding adds no external runtime dependencies.

The fictional board may report strategic momentum. Real release acceptance remains evidence-based.

**Absolutely nothing. Consistently. At scale.**
