# Real project governance | NOTHING™

The fictional company pursues non-delivery. The software project pursues reliability, clarity and reproducibility.

## Authority

The repository owner determines product scope, publication, licensing and final release acceptance. Routine technical and creative decisions may be made within that scope. No fictional appointment, document, score or board resolution confers real permission. Acceptance of the game's direction is not authorisation to expose private source, change repository visibility or grant a licence.

## Change control

Use small reviewable branches and accurate commit messages. Keep real gameplay deterministic; preserve validated saves and offline use. Verify the exact PR head with all quality gates and relevant preview checks before merging. Verify main separately after merging. Record failures and manual limitations honestly; do not substitute old green runs for current evidence. Do not weaken tests to manufacture acceptance. Keep game source, player saves, private editorial material and public release assets within their intended boundaries.

Update relevant current documentation when behaviour, deployment, owner decisions or acceptance change. Use stable workflow links for live status, dated commit-specific links for historical evidence. Preserve historical records rather than rewriting prior claims as though they had always been true.

The owner configured Main Branch Protection with pull requests, status checks, current base, resolved conversations, blocked force pushes and restricted deletions. The ruleset is not enforced while private; follow it voluntarily until enforcement is verified. See [administrative configuration](ADMINISTRATIVE_CONFIGURATION.md).
