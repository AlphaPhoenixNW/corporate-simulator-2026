# PROJECT: CORPORATE SIMULATOR 2026™

## Executive mandate

Design, develop, implement, test and document a complete, polished, playable corporate management simulation game titled Corporate Simulator 2026™: The Pursuit of Strategic Nothingness.

The game is a satirical exploration of corporate bureaucracy, executive incompetence, management consultancy, LinkedIn culture, meaningless productivity metrics and agentic AI hype.

The player assumes the role of CEO of NOTHING™, a multinational corporation whose primary strategic objective is to appear extraordinarily productive while delivering absolutely no measurable value.

## Autonomous execution requirements

- Operate autonomously throughout the development lifecycle.
- Make reasonable architectural, technical and creative decisions independently.
- Do not stop at a prototype, proof of concept, implementation plan or minimum viable product.
- Implement all major systems as functional, interconnected gameplay mechanics.
- Continue through implementation, testing, debugging, refinement and documentation.
- Where practical, delegate independent workstreams to parallel agents.
- Maintain a development backlog and update it throughout implementation.
- Do not request approval for routine technical or creative decisions.
- Work within the current repository and environment without modifying unrelated projects.

## 1. Technical architecture

Build a complete browser-based game using:

- TypeScript
- React
- Vite
- A modular simulation engine
- Persistent local save storage
- Seeded procedural generation
- Responsive desktop and mobile interfaces
- Automated unit, integration and end-to-end testing

Separate the simulation engine from the presentation layer. Establish clear interfaces between gameplay systems and support future extensibility.

The game must run locally without paid APIs, external AI services or subscriptions.

## 2. Core gameplay

Implement a turn-based corporate simulation in which each turn represents one working day.

The player must manage competing organisational metrics:

- Shareholder confidence
- Executive prestige
- Corporate liquidity
- Employee morale
- Bureaucratic complexity
- Perceived productivity
- Actual productivity
- Buzzword density
- Meeting saturation
- Regulatory exposure
- Organisational incompetence

The objective is to maximise perceived productivity, executive compensation and shareholder confidence while keeping actual productivity as close to zero as possible.

Excessive actual productivity should constitute a strategic failure.

## 3. Corporate departments

Implement independently simulated departments with budgets, personnel, objectives, internal politics and procedural events:

- Human Resources
- Finance
- Engineering
- Marketing
- Sales
- Legal and Compliance
- Corporate Strategy
- Digital Transformation
- Artificial Intelligence and Agentic Innovation
- Executive Non-Intervention

Each department must have unique mechanics, interactions and failure modes.

Departments should compete for funding, influence, headcount and executive attention.

## 4. Employee simulation

Generate employees with persistent identities, personality traits, ambitions, skills, salaries, relationships and behavioural tendencies.

Include archetypes such as:

- The Competent Engineer
- The LinkedIn Thought Leader
- The Management Consultant
- The Meeting Enthusiast
- The Agile Evangelist
- The Corporate Survivor
- The Disillusioned Veteran
- The Accidental Innovator
- The Executive Nepotism Hire
- The AI Transformation Specialist

Employees should form alliances, develop rivalries, request promotions, resign, generate internal complaints and occasionally attempt to accomplish useful work.

Competent employees represent a strategic threat and must be managed accordingly.

Implement recruitment, performance reviews, promotions, disciplinary proceedings, redundancies and organisational restructuring.

## 5. Meeting simulator

Develop an extensive meeting system supporting:

- Daily stand-ups
- Executive steering committees
- Strategic alignment workshops
- Pre-meeting alignment meetings
- Post-meeting retrospectives
- Emergency governance meetings
- Meetings to schedule future meetings
- Meetings to investigate excessive meeting frequency

Meetings consume employee time and corporate resources while increasing perceived productivity.

Meeting outcomes should include procedural actions, additional meetings, conflicting recommendations and unnecessary documentation.

## 6. Corporate communications

Implement a procedural communications engine generating:

- Internal emails
- Executive announcements
- Corporate newsletters
- Press releases
- Shareholder statements
- Performance reviews
- Consultancy recommendations
- LinkedIn posts
- Corporate apologies
- Strategic transformation announcements

Create a fictional internal social network where employees congratulate one another on meaningless achievements.

Include engagement metrics, corporate influencers, viral posts and reputational scandals.

Generate coherent satirical content locally through procedural templates and contextual simulation data.

## 7. Enterprise transformation programmes

Allow the player to commission expensive transformation initiatives, including:

- Salesforce Non-Integration
- Cloud-Native Distributed Nothingness
- Quantum-Ready Zero-Outcome Infrastructure
- Enterprise Agile Transformation
- Agentic AI Enablement
- Digital Transformation Transformation

- Bed-Led Leadership
- NOTHING™ OS Enterprise Migration

Each programme must feature budgets, milestones, dependencies, consultancy fees, scope creep, governance committees and opportunities for catastrophic accidental usefulness.

## 8. NOTHING™ OS

Implement a functional fictional operating system within the game.

Include:

- An elaborate animated boot sequence
- A desktop environment
- Corporate applications
- Enterprise settings
- System diagnostics
- Licence management
- Compliance dashboards
- A sophisticated shutdown interface

Its primary functional purpose is to switch itself off.

Successfully shutting down NOTHING™ OS should deliver a 100% reduction in simulated AI token consumption.

Restart functionality should require an expensive enterprise licence.

## 9. Agentic AI platform

Implement a fictional enterprise multi-agent orchestration platform featuring:

- Planner Agent
- Executor Agent
- Critic Agent
- Governance Agent
- Supervisor Agent
- Strategic Non-Intervention Agent

The agents should generate impressive operational reports while attempting to accomplish nothing.

Include simulated token budgets, model selection, inference costs, agentic workflow diagrams, hallucination incidents and executive AI adoption metrics.

All simulation must run locally without real model inference.

- Revenue
- Operating expenditure
- Employee salaries
- Consultancy fees
- Executive bonuses
- Share prices

Implement economic balancing and meaningful financial consequences.

## 11. Procedural events

Create at least 150 distinct procedural corporate events with meaningful choices and consequences.

Examples include:

- An engineer accidentally ships a functional product.
- A consultant recommends hiring additional consultants.
- An executive goes viral on LinkedIn for saying nothing.
- A department completes its objectives ahead of schedule.
- An AI agent demonstrates measurable business value.
- A whistleblower discovers that the company does nothing.
- The board demands a transformation strategy for an existing transformation strategy.
- A shareholder asks what the company actually sells.
- A productivity audit reveals genuine productivity.
- The CEO refuses to get out of bed.

Events should support branching outcomes, delayed consequences and persistent narrative effects.

## 12. Campaign and progression

Implement multiple stages of corporate evolution:

1. Bedroom Startup
2. Seed-Funded Nothingness
3. Venture Capital Darling
4. Enterprise Transformation Partner
5. Multinational Corporation
6. Publicly Listed Organisation
7. Systemically Important Nothingness Provider

Each stage must introduce new mechanics, challenges, departments and opportunities.

Include multiple endings, unlockable scenarios, difficulty settings and an endless sandbox mode.

## 13. Final boss

The final antagonist is an exceptionally competent employee who threatens to deliver measurable organisational value.

The player must neutralise this threat using legitimate corporate gameplay mechanics, including:

- Promotion into senior management
- Committee appointments
- Excessive governance requirements
- Strategic reassignment
- Mandatory training
- Transformation programme leadership

The final confrontation should be a substantial multi-stage gameplay sequence.

Implement:

- Organisational diagrams
- Employee profiles

- Corporate news feeds
- Financial reports
- Achievement animations
- Contextual sound effects
- Original procedurally generated background audio where appropriate
