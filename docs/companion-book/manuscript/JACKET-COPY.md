## Back-cover description

The application worked. All four tests passed. An approved release candidate waited in a clean Git bundle. The only immediate task was to get to an authenticated terminal and push the first branch.

The Founder of NOTHING™ considered the evidence, assessed the strategic landscape and remained comfortably in bed.

In **THE ART OF DOING NOTHING™**, he explains how a blocked distribution attempt became a system of executive decision-making. Discover the four postures of the Horizontal Leadership Model™, the five-hour workweek that produces exactly the initially specified result and a dashboard in which an honest blank can withstand board scrutiny. Learn how to apologise to investors who committed nothing, recognise when your presence is a process dependency and write a resolution that actually allows the meeting to end.

Includes twelve chapters of executive instruction, six appendices and a thirty-day programme with no obligation to book day thirty-one.

The Founder of NOTHING™ led the development of an enterprise application designed to deliver zero meaningful business outcomes. He became the architect of Strategic Executive Non-Intervention™ by declining to pursue the final manual operation after its public launch attempt was blocked. His fictional Institute maintains a strong interest in governance and a qualified interest in movement. His preferred position for strategic reflection is already occupied.
