# Appendix E | Glossary of Corporate Non-Performance Terminology

These are the meanings I use in this book. Present them selectively in conversation. A room may understand one new term and object to being made responsible for fourteen.

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Agentic workflow | Work delegated to an agent with specified authority, evidence and return conditions. At our launch it did substantial preparation and reported the boundary it reached. |
| Aspirational milestone | A future possibility to which no delivery date or commitment has been attached. The term describes the current public launch plan. |
| Bed-Led Organisation™ | My fictional operating model, in which ordinary work and escalation have clear owners and the founder's comfort need not delay every unrelated decision. |
| CLEAR | Contract, Limits, Evidence, Authority and Return. A handoff that makes an approaching boundary visible to the person doing the work. |
| COL | Coverage Operating Limits. The duvet's conceptual operating perimeter, subject to genuine duties and ordinary household conditions. |
| Deferral | A recorded decision to leave an action outstanding under a specified authority or reopening condition. |
| DRAFT | Describe, Reveal, Attribute, Frame and Translate. A method of creating private thought leadership, finished with a sixth closing line. |
| Duvet | A protected resource for executive attention. Depending on circumstances, a household item. |
| EMAI | Executive Movement Avoidance Index. Fictional share of eligible requests resolved without additional physical movement. |
| HOLD | History, Ownership, Limit and Decision. A closing card for a review that could otherwise develop its own calendar. |
| Horizontal Leadership Model™ | A fictional progression from operational overcommitment through uncertainty and clearer delegation to a state in which the executive can leave without creating a new dependency. |
| N/A | No applicable denominator. A display state requiring substantially more confidence than the colour green. |
| Outcome-Neutral Performance™ | Verified preservation of an intentionally specified zero practical outcome within a defined scope. |
| Release gate | A condition or authority that must be satisfied for distribution to proceed. Passing a product test provides evidence about the product. |
| SEV-0 | In this work, the private project's satirical designation for the inaugural failure to deliver nothing. |
| SEN | Stakeholder Expectation Neutrality. Fictional share of evidenced audience groups whose stated commitments match actual status. |
| SHA | Strategic Horizontal Alignment in the fictional dashboard. Engineering retains its separate use of those letters when discussing hashes. |
| STAY | Scope, Trigger, Authority and Yield. A diagnostic card for a proposed intervention. |
| Strategic Executive Non-Intervention™ | Deliberate preservation of executive attention through elimination of unnecessary operational participation. |
| Successfully Not Launched | The recorded disposition of the public launch attempt. It leaves the private artefact and its verification intact. |
| UASR | Unnecessary Action Suppression Ratio. Fictional share of assessed unnecessary proposals formally retired before execution. |
| ZODR | Zero Outcome Delivery Rate. Fictional share of planned eligible zero-outcome executions that were actually observed and verified. |
