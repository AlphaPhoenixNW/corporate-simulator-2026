# Appendix A | The NOTHING™ Leadership Manifesto

I drafted these articles for a leader who needs to resolve a question without becoming a permanent part of its answer. Use whichever articles help you. The Institute does not require a signature. It has no operational interest in chasing signatures from people who wish to prove their ability to avoid unnecessary work.

## Article I | Choose the outcome before allocating the labour

I begin with the change somebody expects to observe. If the expectation is zero, I record that expectation before opening the template. A beautifully formatted benefits section will otherwise begin to suggest what it is owed. I prefer to leave it an empty field with excellent prospects for retirement.

## Article II | Give engineering a test it can pass

I ask for evidence of what the product can do. I give engineering credit when that evidence appears. I resist the opportunity to borrow its success for another gate. A finished application can remain unavailable. I am qualified to accept both states, even if I must hold them in separate hands.

## Article III | Require the interruption to declare its purpose

I accept interruptions that contain a specific decision or a live obligation. I am prepared to receive these in many different postures. I ask all other interruptions to identify how my presence would alter their result. Where they can find nothing, their contact with my office has already reached an appropriate conclusion.

## Article IV | Delegate authority in a size the recipient can carry

I want a delegate to understand the permission they have and the permission they need. Someone authorised to prepare an artefact may be unable to move it. Someone able to move it may need separate approval to release it. I ask each person to report the real boundary and refrain from making it their personal failing.

## Article V | Be able to name the person responsible for nothing happening

Where I choose to defer, I own that choice. The existence of a technical obstacle can explain why we arrived at a decision. It cannot decide on my behalf whether we should pursue another route. An executive who wishes to claim the wisdom of restraint should be willing to accept the practical state in which the decision leaves the project.

## Article VI | Permit rest to retain its original character

I reject the argument that every available interval should be converted into an advantage for the enterprise. It may fund the enterprise more reliably by funding the person. The duvet need produce neither a proposal nor a performance claim. I have found that allowing it to be a duvet provides considerable clarity about what exists on either side of it.

## Article VII | Close what can be closed

I write down what occurred, who may reopen it and whether anything remains owed. If the record lets a person go home, I allow it to do so. A successful stopping condition is already a kind of infrastructure. It requires very little additional work to prove it is there.

## Adoption exercise

Choose one article and describe a decision in which it would have applied. Bring evidence of what occurred. If you decide that the article would have changed no relevant part of the decision, put it back. This manifesto has reached maturity when a reader feels no obligation to adopt it.
