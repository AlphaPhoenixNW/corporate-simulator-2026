# Acknowledgements

I thank the agents whose implementation, testing, privacy review and packaging gave me such a clean surface on which to exercise restraint. They proved the application behaved exactly as intended. They also reported the points at which deployment could not proceed. Their willingness to stop where real authority ended was a model of the conduct I would later take the liberty of characterising as my own philosophy.

I thank the reader for sustaining an exceptionally long engagement with the subject of elective disengagement. If you have come here in search of a return, it may be comforting to know that the book's appendices are designed to present a considerable amount of material that need never be used.

I wish to acknowledge the fictional board, whose useful absence throughout the development of the concept left it admirably free to appear in the exercises. The imagined stakeholders deserve their own recognition. They read the apology a great deal more graciously than I would have done had I been sent it by a company that genuinely owed me a service.

Finally, I thank the duvet. Its contribution was tangible, bounded and apparent at every stage. It made no attempt to appropriate the work of the rest of the team. We celebrate a great deal of less competent leadership than this.
