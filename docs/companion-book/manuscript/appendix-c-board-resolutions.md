# Appendix C | Sample Board Resolutions for Outcome-Neutral Organisations

The following are fictional model resolutions. I keep them separate from the project's board memorandum, which is a proposal to a simulated board and records no vote. Adapt one only when an actual decision requires it. I maintain that unused resolution templates are an unusually inexpensive way to signal institutional capacity.

## Resolution C.1 | Acceptance of a verified artefact

> Resolved, that the board receives the verified artefact and recognises the testing attested in its delivery record. The release decision remains with its named authority. The minutes may record readiness for local use and the actual state of distribution.

Use this text when a team has completed work whose consequence depends on separate approval. A member may ask whether the resolution could simply say that the product is available. Refer them to the last sentence. If the last sentence is proving inadequate, provide the member with a copy of the destination record and time in which to read it.

## Resolution C.2 | Preservation of the present release state

> Resolved, that the release is deferred until a fresh owner decision. The custodian shall retain the approved source and verification evidence. Work shall resume upon identification of an authorised operator, a checked destination and an instruction to reopen planning.

This is suitable for a case in which obligations permit deferral. The purpose of retaining the artefact is to preserve a choice. The purpose of describing the trigger is to prevent the custodian becoming a full-time observer of the choice.

## Resolution C.3 | Recognition of limited executive participation

> Resolved, that routine work may proceed under the authority of its responsible roles. The founder shall be approached for matters requiring owner authority and for escalations already specified by the operating plan. Physical presence carries no separate approval power.

If your founder happens to attend a meeting voluntarily, please allow them to maintain the fiction that they came because they wanted to. The measure has been written to protect accountability, not to drive the founder out of any room containing a chair.

## Resolution C.4 | Adjournment without a further committee

> Resolved, that the present questions have dispositions and named owners. The board adjourns. Further consideration requires new evidence or a decision within the board's remit.

I recommend reading this aloud once. Every additional reading should be treated as a procedural error. In the last simulation a director requested an extraordinary meeting to ratify the adjournment. The chair invited him to write down the decision the meeting would make. On receipt of the answer, she handled it by correspondence of exceptional brevity.

## Approval record for the exercise

| Field | What to write |
| --- | --- |
| Authority | Which fictional or real body would decide |
| Decision | The resolution actually considered |
| Evidence | The facts the body relied upon |
| Disposition | Approved, deferred or declined |
| Reopening trigger | A new circumstance that warrants review |

A template records a decision only after somebody with authority has made one. I once placed entirely blank approval records into a sample board pack. Their restraint was universally admired. I took care to record that sentiment outside the approval fields.
