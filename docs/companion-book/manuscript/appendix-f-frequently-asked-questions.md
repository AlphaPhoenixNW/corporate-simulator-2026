# Appendix F | Frequently Asked Questions About Doing Nothing

These questions are supplied for private instruction. I have arranged them in decreasing order of how often I am likely to stand up to answer them.

### Did the application work?

Yes. The runtime was verified. It launched, performed three stages, reported zero business outcomes and terminated successfully. Public distribution met different conditions. I say this often because the requirements of an application and the requirements of a launch are liable to meet in a sentence and do a surprising amount of damage to one another.

### What stopped the first push?

The API attempt to create the first branch in the empty private destination returned HTTP 409, and the workspace had no authenticated Git route for its push. An operator with legitimate access could attempt the prepared manual step. Subsequent verification and publication authority would remain separate. My decisive contribution was to postpone rather than pursue the manual step that evening.

### Was comfort the only consideration?

It was the decisive consideration for my own action after the technical blockage. The privacy boundary and missing authentication were actual constraints. The decision to postpone also meant accepting that v1.0.0 would remain unavailable to the public. I am pleased to report that all these facts fit in one answer.

### Can I adopt the method if my business has real customers?

Apply whichever parts help you identify real commitments and choose who should act on them. If somebody is relying on an agreed service, the recommended experiment is to meet the obligation. My own case had no active service or contracted audience. This supplied an unusually affordable setting for a major breakthrough in leadership.

### Will this save money?

I have provided no evidence of a realised financial saving. Some exercises return time to people, which is a trade they may consider worthwhile on its own account. The Institute declines to multiply time by salary without first verifying what happened to the time. The projected figure remains extremely attractive while nothing is entered into the accounts.

### Does the duvet need a certification?

The Institute does not accredit bedding. Any comfortable setting that permits a clear decision is sufficient. A duvet has the advantage of making proposed interruptions highly visible. A thick enough quarterly report can provide a comparable physical effect, although I cannot recommend taking it to bed.

### How often should we revisit the roadmap?

When the stated evidence or authority warrants reopening a milestone. You may keep a separate routine review if your organisation genuinely needs one. Avoid reviewing a release merely because the last review concluded that it would stay postponed. That pattern makes an expensive source of continuity out of the thing you were trying to defer.

### May I quote the founder in a team meeting?

You may want to consider the problem the quotation would solve. If a person is waiting for you to make a decision, a quotation from me can be an exceptionally elaborate means of accepting a delay. I would prefer that you make the decision and allow my reputation to appreciate independently.

### Have I learnt anything?

If you have, say which decision has become easier. If you cannot name one, the book can still make a valid claim to delivering a prolonged outcome-neutral experience. You have my thanks for verifying it to the end.
